# Tunga Bank KYC, Nominees and Joint Accounts

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## KYC documents

Accepted proofs of identity and address are Aadhaar, passport, voter ID and driving licence. PAN is mandatory for any account, or Form 60 if the customer has no PAN.

## Periodic KYC update

High-risk customers must update KYC every 2 years, medium-risk every 8 years and low-risk every 10 years. If KYC is not updated within 90 days of the reminder, the account is restricted to credits only.

## Video KYC

Video KYC is available from 9 AM to 9 PM on all days. The customer needs their original PAN card and a phone with a working camera.

## Nominee

Each savings account and FD can have up to 4 nominees, with the share split in percentages that add up to 100. Nominees can be changed any time from the app at no cost.

## Joint accounts

Joint accounts can have up to 4 holders. Operating modes are either-or-survivor, former-or-survivor, and jointly. Changing the operating mode needs signatures from all holders.

## Deceased claimant process

With a nominee registered, the bank settles the claim within 15 days of receiving the death certificate and the nominee's KYC. Without a nominee, the legal heirs need a succession certificate for balances above Rs 15 lakh.
