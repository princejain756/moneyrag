# Tunga Bank Mutual Fund and SIP Desk

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only. Nothing here is investment advice.

## Starting a SIP

The smallest SIP through Tunga Bank is Rs 500 a month. SIP dates available are the 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th and 25th. The first instalment is debited within 7 working days of registration, and later ones on the chosen date.

## Missed SIP instalments

If the account does not have enough money on the SIP date, the instalment is skipped and the bank charges a bounce fee of Rs 250. After 3 consecutive missed instalments the SIP is cancelled by the fund house.

## Pausing and stopping

A SIP can be paused for 1 to 6 months from the app. Stopping a SIP needs 10 working days of notice before the next debit.

## Exit load on equity funds

Most equity funds sold through the desk charge a 1% exit load if units are redeemed within 365 days of purchase. Each SIP instalment counts as a separate purchase with its own 365 day clock.

## Redemption timeline

Equity fund redemptions reach the bank account within 3 working days. Liquid fund redemptions arrive the next working day.

## Distributor commission

Tunga Bank earns a trail commission from fund houses on regular plans. Customers who want no commission can buy direct plans from the fund house instead.
