# Tunga Bank Disputes, Chargebacks and Fraud

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Reporting unauthorised transactions

Customers should report an unauthorised electronic transaction as soon as possible. If reported within 3 working days, the customer's liability is zero. Reported within 4 to 7 working days, liability is capped at Rs 10,000 for savings accounts and Rs 25,000 for credit cards above Rs 5 lakh limit. After 7 working days liability depends on the bank's board-approved policy.

## Shadow credit

For valid fraud reports, the bank credits the disputed amount to the account within 10 working days as a shadow credit while it investigates.

## Card chargeback

A card dispute for goods not received or a wrong amount must be raised within 120 days of the transaction. The bank sends the chargeback to the card network within 5 working days. The final outcome is usually known in 45 to 90 days.

## Blocking a card

The card can be blocked instantly in the app, by SMS BLOCK to the registered number, or by calling the 24x7 helpline. Blocking is free. A replacement debit card costs Rs 200 and a replacement credit card is free.

## Complaints and ombudsman

If a complaint is not resolved in 30 days or the customer is unhappy with the reply, they can escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer and then to the banking ombudsman.
