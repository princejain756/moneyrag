# Tunga Bank Debit Card

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Card variants

The Classic debit card has an annual fee of Rs 150 and an ATM withdrawal limit of Rs 25,000 a day. The Platinum card has an annual fee of Rs 450 and a daily ATM limit of Rs 1 lakh. Shopping limits are Rs 1 lakh and Rs 3 lakh a day respectively.

## Contactless payments

Tap to pay works without a PIN for payments up to Rs 5,000. Above that the PIN is needed. Contactless can be switched off in the app.

## International usage

International use is off by default and can be switched on in the app for a chosen period. Foreign ATM withdrawals cost Rs 150 each plus a 3.5% currency markup.

## Failed ATM withdrawal

If cash is not dispensed but the account is debited, the amount is reversed within 5 calendar days. For every day of delay after that, the bank pays Rs 100 to the customer automatically.

## Insurance cover

Platinum cards include personal accident cover of Rs 5 lakh, valid only if the card was used at least once in the 90 days before the accident.
