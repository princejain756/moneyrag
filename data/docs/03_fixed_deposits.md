# Tunga Bank Fixed Deposits

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Interest rates

General public rates per year: 7 to 45 days 3.5%, 46 to 179 days 5.25%, 180 days to under 1 year 6.0%, 1 year to under 2 years 7.1%, 2 years to under 5 years 7.0%, 5 to 10 years 6.5%. Senior citizens (60 and above) get an extra 0.5% on every tenure.

## Minimum deposit

The minimum FD amount is Rs 5,000. Deposits of Rs 3 crore or more are bulk deposits and get rates quoted separately.

## Premature withdrawal

Breaking an FD before maturity pays the rate applicable for the period the money actually stayed, minus a 1% penalty. FDs broken within 7 days earn no interest. There is no penalty on FDs of Rs 5 lakh or less that are broken to fund a medical emergency, with hospital proof.

## Tax saver FD

The 5-year tax saver FD has a lock-in of 5 years, no premature withdrawal and no loan against it. The maximum per financial year is Rs 1.5 lakh.

## Auto renewal

Unless the customer opts out, a maturing FD renews for the same tenure at the rate on the renewal date. Interest payout FDs renew only the principal.

## Loan against FD

Customers can borrow up to 90% of the FD value. The loan rate is the FD rate plus 1%.
