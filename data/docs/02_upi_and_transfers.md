# Tunga Bank UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## UPI limits

The UPI limit is Rs 1 lakh per transaction and Rs 1 lakh per day across all UPI apps linked to the account. For tax payments, hospital bills and education fees to verified merchants, the per-transaction limit rises to Rs 5 lakh. A new device can send at most Rs 5,000 in its first 24 hours.

## UPI failures and reversals

If a UPI payment is debited but the merchant does not receive it, the amount is auto-reversed within T+1 working day. If the reversal does not happen, the bank pays compensation of Rs 100 per day of delay after T+1, credited without a claim.

## IMPS

IMPS works 24x7 with a limit of Rs 5 lakh per transaction. Charges are Rs 0 up to Rs 1,000, Rs 2.5 from Rs 1,001 to Rs 1 lakh, and Rs 5 above Rs 1 lakh, plus GST.

## NEFT

NEFT runs in half-hourly batches, 24x7, including holidays. Online NEFT from the app is free. NEFT done at a branch costs Rs 2 up to Rs 10,000, Rs 4 up to Rs 1 lakh, Rs 14 up to Rs 2 lakh and Rs 24 above that.

## RTGS

RTGS is for Rs 2 lakh and above, with no upper limit set by the bank. Online RTGS is free. Branch RTGS costs Rs 20 for Rs 2 to 5 lakh and Rs 45 above Rs 5 lakh.

## Adding a beneficiary

A newly added beneficiary can receive at most Rs 50,000 in the first 24 hours. The cooling period protects customers if their login is stolen.
