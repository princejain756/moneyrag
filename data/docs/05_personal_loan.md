# Tunga Bank Personal Loan

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Eligibility

Salaried applicants aged 21 to 58 with a net monthly income of at least Rs 25,000 can apply. Self-employed applicants need two years of income tax returns.

## Interest rate and fees

Rates range from 10.75% to 21% a year, set by credit score and income. The processing fee is 2% of the loan amount, minimum Rs 1,999, plus GST.

## Tenure and amount

Loans run from 12 to 60 months. The amount ranges from Rs 50,000 to Rs 25 lakh.

## Prepayment and foreclosure

Part prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs, up to 25% of the outstanding principal once a year, with a charge of 2%. Full foreclosure after 12 EMIs costs 3% of the outstanding principal. Floating rate loans to individuals have no foreclosure charge.

## Missed EMI

A bounced EMI costs Rs 550 per instance plus 2% a month penal charge on the overdue amount. The bank reports defaults to credit bureaus after 30 days past due.

## Cooling-off period

Borrowers can exit the loan within 3 days of disbursal by repaying the principal and the proportionate interest, with no penalty. The processing fee is refunded except Rs 999.
