# Tunga Bank Neem Credit Card: Fees and Terms

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Annual fee

The joining fee is Rs 499. The annual fee of Rs 499 is waived if the cardholder spends Rs 1.5 lakh or more in the card year.

## Interest and grace period

The interest-free period is 20 to 50 days, depending on when in the billing cycle the purchase was made. Unpaid balances are charged 3.6% a month (43.2% a year). Interest is charged from the transaction date once the full amount is not paid by the due date.

## Minimum amount due

The minimum amount due is 5% of the total outstanding or Rs 200, whichever is higher, plus any EMI instalments and overlimit amount.

## Late payment fee

Late fee depends on the statement balance: nil up to Rs 500, Rs 100 for Rs 501 to Rs 5,000, Rs 500 for Rs 5,001 to Rs 20,000, and Rs 950 above Rs 20,000. A late fee is charged only if the minimum amount due is not paid within 3 days of the due date.

## Cash advance

Cash withdrawal on the card costs 2.5% of the amount, minimum Rs 500. Cash advances have no interest-free period. The cash limit is 40% of the credit limit.

## Foreign currency markup

Transactions in a foreign currency, or in rupees with a merchant based outside India, carry a 3.5% markup plus GST.

## Rewards

The card earns 2 reward points per Rs 150 spent. Fuel, rent and wallet loads earn no points. 1 reward point is worth Rs 0.25 when redeemed against the statement.
