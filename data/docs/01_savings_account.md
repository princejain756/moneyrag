# Tunga Bank Savings Account: Charges and Rules

All documents in this folder describe Tunga Bank, a fictional bank made up for testing. None of these numbers are real bank policy.

## Minimum balance

Regular savings accounts must keep a monthly average balance (MAB) of Rs 10,000 in metro branches and Rs 5,000 in semi-urban branches. Rural branches need Rs 2,000. The MAB is the sum of each day's closing balance divided by the number of days in the month.

## Penalty for low balance

If the MAB falls short, the bank charges 6% of the shortfall, capped at Rs 500 per month. The charge is debited on the 5th of the next month. The penalty never takes the balance below zero.

## Interest on savings

Savings interest is 2.75% a year on balances up to Rs 1 lakh and 3.25% on the portion above Rs 1 lakh. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly, in the last week of March, June, September and December.

## Free transactions

Each month includes 5 free cash withdrawals at Tunga Bank ATMs and 3 free withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in the six metro cities. After that, each cash withdrawal costs Rs 21 and each balance enquiry at another bank's ATM costs Rs 8.

## Account closure

Closing an account within 14 days of opening is free. Closing between 15 days and 12 months costs Rs 500. After 12 months closure is free. The balance is paid out by NEFT to a linked account or by demand draft.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months becomes dormant. Interest keeps being credited. To reactivate, the customer submits fresh KYC at any branch or through video KYC.
