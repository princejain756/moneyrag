# Tunga Bank Home Loan

Fictional bank, synthetic policy for testing only.

## Loan to value

The bank lends up to 90% of the property value for loans up to Rs 30 lakh, 80% for loans from Rs 30 lakh to Rs 75 lakh, and 75% above Rs 75 lakh. Stamp duty and registration are not counted in the property value.

## Interest rate

Home loans are floating, linked to the repo rate. The current spread puts rates at 8.4% to 9.6% a year based on credit score. Women borrowers get 0.05% off.

## Tenure

The maximum tenure is 30 years, and the loan must end before the borrower turns 70.

## Processing fee

The processing fee is 0.5% of the loan amount, capped at Rs 10,000, plus GST.

## Prepayment

Floating rate home loans to individuals have no prepayment or foreclosure charges. When a borrower prepays, they choose whether to cut the EMI or the tenure.

## Balance transfer

Customers moving a home loan from another lender need 12 months of clean repayment history. The bank waives the processing fee on balance transfers above Rs 50 lakh.
